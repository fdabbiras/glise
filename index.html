01 / About

Still
figuring it out.

Fashion has always felt like a playground for me. I'm not really interested in wearing something just because it's trending. I like finding pieces that feel different, slightly unexpected, or simply make me curious.

My style naturally gravitates toward vintage fashion, clean and chic silhouettes, unusual statement pieces, unexpected colors, and details that make people look twice.

I'm still developing my voice as a creator, experimenting with styling, visual storytelling, and beauty while discovering what makes fashion feel uniquely mine.

— Denis
